## Why
Photos with the same name in one folder (`R0001.RAF` and `R0001.JPG`) are one photo. The record stores the companion keys, but the viewer does not show them.

## What to do
- Include companion file names in the month file.
- Viewer shows a small list: RAW, camera JPEG, size.
- Download buttons per file.

## Done when
- A RAW+JPEG pair shows both files and downloads each.
