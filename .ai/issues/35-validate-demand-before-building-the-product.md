## Why
Pricing, audience and positioning in `docs/product/` are hypotheses. The main unproven assumption is willingness to pay.

## What to do
- Interview 30 RAW-shooting photographers.
- Put up a landing page with the plans and a pre-order.
- Run a paid concierge beta with about 25 people.
- Run a Van Westendorp price survey.

## Done when
- At least 30% of interviewed target users say they would pay the Pro price, and at least 10 of 25 beta users convert.
